4 · Rules

  • Accessible blue #1D4ED8 owns primary actions, links, focus rings (6.70:1 with white). Brand blue #3B82F6 owns large graphics, fills, selected states — never body text (3.68:1).
  • Accessible green #15803D carries success and attestation text (5.02:1). Brand green #4FD166 is fill-only with ink text #052E16 (7.56:1) — never green on white (1.97:1).
  • Never color alone: pair status with icon + text + scope. "Verified" says what was checked, by whom, when.
  • Dark ink #0B1220 on brand-blue fills (5.09:1); white text only on accessible blue/green.
  • Hero gradient only: 135deg, #1E3A8A → #1D4ED8 → #15803D. 60/30/10.
  • Spacing 4-pt base, 8-pt rhythm · touch targets 48 · radius 12 buttons / 16 cards · motion 150–300ms, reduce-motion respected.
  • Full spec: doc/brand-system.md · tokens: brand-tokens.css · Flutter: example/brand_theme.dart.
