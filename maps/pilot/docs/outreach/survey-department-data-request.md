## Email subject

Request for official boundary geometry and permission for a separate bilingual atlas edition

## Email body

Dear Director General,

I am writing on behalf of the **Nepal Local Level Atlas** project, a bilingual (Nepali and English) reference atlas of Nepal's provinces, districts and local levels. The current open-data pilot is a non-commercial publication delivered as printable PDF sheets and an offline web map. It uses generalised open-data geometry and states clearly that it is not survey-grade and not a legal boundary determination.

We are planning a **separate, permission-dependent edition** based on official Survey Department geometry. This edition would be visually and semantically distinct from the open-data pilot. We are not asking to mix official geometry into the open-data edition.

We would like to request the following.

### 1. Available boundary data

- Province, district and local-level boundaries, in the department's current official version.
- Ward-level boundaries, if available.
- The corresponding attribute tables, including codes and official names in Nepali and English.
- Metadata: coordinate reference system, version or survey date, accuracy statement and any generalisation applied.

Preferred formats are Esri Shapefile, File Geodatabase or GeoJSON. If a different format is standard, we will work with it.

### 2. Permission and conditions

- Permission to reproduce the geometry in a **print and offline digital edition**, with attribution.
- Whether the edition may be distributed publicly, and under what conditions.
- Whether a downloadable data bundle is permitted, or whether the edition must be print-only or view-only.
- The required attribution, citation and disclaimer.
- Any fee, licence agreement, data-sharing form or internal approval process.

### 3. Ward-level coverage

We understand ward-level geometry and authoritative ward tables may not be publicly available. If they can be released for this edition, please indicate the format, cost and conditions. If not, we will keep ward coverage out of scope.

### 4. Official peak or elevation data

If the department publishes an official peak or elevation dataset, we would be grateful for the citation and any conditions of use. This is secondary to the boundary request.

### 5. Contact and process

Please let us know the correct application route, the responsible office, and the expected timeline. If a formal letter or an in-person request is required, we will follow that process.

We will not describe the resulting edition as survey-grade unless the department confirms that wording, and we will display the department's attribution and disclaimer exactly as required.

Thank you for considering this request.
