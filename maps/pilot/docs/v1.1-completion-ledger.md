## 5. What the DNPWC-deferred edition contains

**In scope**

- 51 peak points: 8 main eight-thousanders, 6 official additional summits, 37 seven-thousanders.
- Bilingual peak labels, class-specific symbols, and the 8 vs 14 status note.
- Protected-area and buffer-zone **attribute tables**: 22 core/provincial rows and 14 buffer rows, with published area, designation, authority, dates, province/district and a “boundary pending” note.
- `data/reference/reference_tables.json` — the validated non-spatial export of those 36 rows, generated at G3.
- Web reference panel and print reference tables.
- Print sheet 04 (A2 landscape, peaks) and the 14 eight-thousanders on the national sheet.
- Clear separation between administrative and reference information.

**Out of scope until DNPWC permission**

- Any protected-area or buffer-zone polygon.
- Any buffer-zone hatch overlay on the map.
- Any implied spatial extent for Chhayanath or Badimalika–Ramaroshan.
- Any use of WDPA, `stepps00` or OSM geometry as a substitute.

**Labels that must appear**

- “Published area; boundary pending.”
- “Attribute-only record under D10; not a legal boundary.”
- “Not survey-grade. Not a legal boundary determination.”
- “The 5,720.28 km² buffer total is the sum of 14 published rows, not an official aggregate.”
- “5,602 km² covers the 12 buffer zones declared 1996–2010.”
